# How to Make a Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions

- You have two slices of bread.
- You have peanut butter and jelly.
- You have a butter knife.
- You are making one sandwich.
- All ingredients are safe to eat and ready to use.

## Steps

1. Place one slice of bread flat on a clean plate.
2. Use the butter knife to spread peanut butter evenly over one side of the bread.
3. Place the knife down and use it to spread jelly evenly over the peanut butter.
4. Pick up the second slice of bread.
5. Place the second slice of bread on top of the first slice, with the jelly side facing the first slice.
6. Press the two slices of bread together gently.
7. Cut the sandwich in half if desired.
8. Place the finished sandwich on the plate.

**Done When:** The two slices of bread are together with peanut butter and jelly between them, and the sandwich is ready to eat.
